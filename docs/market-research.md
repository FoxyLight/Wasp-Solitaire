# Market and competitor research

**Status:** Research draft; not a validated business forecast  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-29

## Scope and caution

This is an initial desk assessment of dedicated Wasp titles, related variants, and mainstream Solitaire UI benchmarks. Public listing details (especially ratings, downloads, prices, ads, and reviews) change; recheck immediately before any commercial decision. No hands-on comparative usability test has been performed.

## Differentiation hypotheses

1. **Purpose-built Wasp:** simpler navigation than a multi-game collection.
2. **Readable seven-column layout:** retain useful rank/suit visibility on phones.
3. **Predictable controls:** drag or two-step tap choice; visible legal moves.
4. **Offline-first privacy:** no accounts or connectivity required.
5. **No disruptive ads in the initial product proposal:** distinguish via uninterrupted gameplay.
6. **Reliable session recovery:** foreground/background, rotation, crash and restart stability.

None of these is proven to increase installs or retention yet.

## Review research method for the next pass

For each current competitor: record latest app version/date, locale, developer, monetization, screenshots, downloads/rating snapshot, notable positive/negative review themes, and a hands-on test of move input, undo, autosave, accessibility and ads. Preserve dated screenshots or evidence links with copyright-safe summaries. Separate one-off reviews from repeated themes; do not extrapolate frequency from a small visible review sample.

## Key research gaps

- Evidence of dedicated Wasp user demand and willingness to pay.
- True frequency of gesture/readability complaints.
- Solvability statistics using a reproducible solver.
- Pricing/monetization sensitivity.
- First-party usability testing on narrow phones and with assistive technology.

## Recommendation

Build a focused high-quality MVP before features such as subscriptions, online challenges or multi-variant catalogs. Revisit monetization only after real-user feedback and Play policy assessment.
